Questions people ask

Still unsure? Write to the admissions team.

Do I need to take an entrance exam?

Most programs admit on profile alone. The PG diploma adds a 45-minute online aptitude test, and some programs have a short interview.

Is there an age limit?

No. Our learners range from final-year students to senior executives.

Can I pay the fee in instalments?

Yes. Programs above ₹50,000 can be paid in up to three instalments at no extra cost.

Who issues the certificate?

Certificates are issued by the Centre for Continuing Education, IIT Hyderabad, and can be verified online.

How many hours a week should I plan for?

Between 4 and 12 hours, depending on the program. Each program page lists its weekly effort.

What do I need to join live classes?

A laptop or desktop with a webcam and a stable 5 Mbps internet connection. Labs for each program list any additional requirements.
