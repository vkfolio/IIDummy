Documents you will need

  • Degree certificate or provisional certificate
  • Mark sheets or transcripts for your highest degree
  • A government photo ID (Aadhaar, PAN or passport)
  • A recent photograph
  • Experience letter, if the program asks for work experience
  • Category certificate, if you are claiming a fee concession

PDF, JPG or PNG, up to 5 MB each. You can save your application and upload documents later.

Fees, scholarships and eligibility

Scholarships of up to 50% are available on selected programs.

Do I need to take an entrance exam?

Most programs admit on profile alone. The PG diploma adds a 45-minute online aptitude test, and some programs have a short interview.

Is there an age limit?

No. Our learners range from final-year students to senior executives.

Can I pay the fee in instalments?

Yes. Programs above ₹50,000 can be paid in up to three instalments at no extra cost.

What is the refund policy?

You get a full refund, minus the application fee, if you withdraw before the program starts. After that, refunds are pro-rated for the first two weeks.

Are scholarships available?

Yes. Merit scholarships, scholarships for women in technology, and discounts for IITH alumni and group enrolments are available. They are listed on each program page.
