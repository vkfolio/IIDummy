About the program

The diploma combines live online teaching with three week-long residencies on the IIT Hyderabad campus. It is designed for people who want to move into data roles or lead data teams.

Every term ends with a project on a real dataset, and the final term is a supervised capstone.

What you will be able to do

  • Clean, model and explain real-world data
  • Build and evaluate predictive models
  • Design data pipelines that scale
  • Present findings to non-technical audiences

Eligibility

  • A bachelor’s degree with mathematics or statistics at 12th grade level, with at least 60% or 6.0 CGPA.
  • Applicants are shortlisted on profile and a 45-minute online aptitude test.
