About the program

Built around your own venture. Each month covers a stage of building a deep-tech company, with mentors from IITH’s incubators and founders from its alumni network.

What you will be able to do

  • Validate a market for a technology
  • Build a go-to-market plan
  • Raise early-stage funding

Eligibility

  • A bachelor’s degree and either a technology idea or a role in corporate innovation.
