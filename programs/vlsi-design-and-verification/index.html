About the program

India is building a semiconductor industry, and it needs designers and verification engineers. This program covers the full digital design flow using the same tools used by chip companies.

Remote access to EDA tools is provided throughout, and a two-week lab residency at IIT Hyderabad covers tape-out preparation.

Industry EDA tools
Remote licences for the full design and verification flow.
Lab residency
Two weeks in IITH’s VLSI labs.
Aligned with the India Semiconductor Mission
Curriculum reviewed with industry partners.

What you will be able to do

  • Write synthesizable RTL in Verilog and SystemVerilog
  • Build UVM testbenches
  • Run synthesis, timing analysis and place-and-route

Who should apply

Electronics and electrical engineering graduates, and engineers moving into chip design.

Questions about this program

Will I get an IIT Hyderabad certificate?

Yes. Learners who meet the attendance and assessment requirements receive a certificate issued by the Centre for Continuing Education, IIT Hyderabad.

Can I attend if I miss a live class?

Every live session is recorded and available on the learning platform within 24 hours.

Is there an instalment option?

Yes. You can pay in up to three instalments without extra charge. Details are in your offer letter.
