About the program

Built for senior managers who will make the calls on AI investments. You will learn enough of how the technology works to ask the right questions, and spend most of the time on cases, risk and organisational change.

Ten Friday evenings
Two-hour live sessions designed around a working week.
Indian case studies
Banking, healthcare, manufacturing and the public sector.
Peer cohort
Small cohort of directors, CXOs and founders.

What you will be able to do

  • Spot high-value, low-risk use cases
  • Build a business case and adoption roadmap
  • Set up AI governance and risk review

Who should apply

Managers and leaders with at least eight years of work experience.

Questions about this program

Will I get an IIT Hyderabad certificate?

Yes. Learners who meet the attendance and assessment requirements receive a certificate issued by the Centre for Continuing Education, IIT Hyderabad.

Can I attend if I miss a live class?

Every live session is recorded and available on the learning platform within 24 hours.

Is there an instalment option?

Yes. You can pay in up to three instalments without extra charge. Details are in your offer letter.
