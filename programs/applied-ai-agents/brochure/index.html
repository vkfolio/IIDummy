About the program

Most AI demos break the moment real users touch them. This program is about the engineering that comes after the demo: grounding agents in your data, giving them tools, measuring whether they are right, and keeping them safe and affordable in production.

Each module ends with a lab you build on your own laptop and a review from teaching assistants. By the capstone you will have shipped an agent for a real workflow from your organisation or one of ours.

What you will be able to do

  • Build retrieval-augmented agents over your own documents and databases
  • Design tool-calling and multi-step agent workflows that fail safely
  • Evaluate agents with test suites, LLM judges and human review
  • Deploy and monitor agents with cost, latency and safety budgets

Eligibility

  • A bachelor’s degree in engineering, science, mathematics or computer applications, with at least 55% or 5.5 CGPA.
  • At least one year of programming experience in Python. No machine learning background is required.
  • Final-year undergraduate students may apply and join if they graduate before the program starts.

Curriculum

  1. 1Foundations of large language models. How LLMs work, where they fail, and prompting as interface design.Weeks 1 to 3
  2. 2Retrieval and grounding. Connect models to your own knowledge, safely and measurably.Weeks 4 to 7
  3. 3Tools and agents. Let models act: tool calling, planning and multi-agent patterns.Weeks 8 to 12
  4. 4Evaluation and safety. Know whether your agent is right before your users do.Weeks 13 to 17
  5. 5Production and capstone. Deploy, monitor and present a capstone agent.Weeks 18 to 24
