About the program

Most AI demos break the moment real users touch them. This program is about the engineering that comes after the demo: grounding agents in your data, giving them tools, measuring whether they are right, and keeping them safe and affordable in production.

Each module ends with a lab you build on your own laptop and a review from teaching assistants. By the capstone you will have shipped an agent for a real workflow from your organisation or one of ours.

Weekend live classes
Saturday and Sunday mornings, so you can keep working full time.
12 guided labs
Hands-on builds with code reviews from IITH teaching assistants.
Capstone with industry mentors
Ship an agent for a real workflow and present it to a panel.
Two days on campus
Optional immersion at IIT Hyderabad, Kandi, at the end of the program.

What you will be able to do

  • Build retrieval-augmented agents over your own documents and databases
  • Design tool-calling and multi-step agent workflows that fail safely
  • Evaluate agents with test suites, LLM judges and human review
  • Deploy and monitor agents with cost, latency and safety budgets

Who should apply

Software engineers, data scientists and technical leads with at least one year of programming experience in Python.

Curriculum

  1. 1Foundations of large language modelsWeeks 1 to 3+

    How LLMs work, where they fail, and prompting as interface design.

    • Tokens and context windows
    • Prompt patterns
    • Structured outputs
  2. 2Retrieval and groundingWeeks 4 to 7+

    Connect models to your own knowledge, safely and measurably.

    • Embeddings and vector search
    • Chunking strategies
    • Citations and grounding checks
  3. 3Tools and agentsWeeks 8 to 12+

    Let models act: tool calling, planning and multi-agent patterns.

    • Tool design
    • Agent loops
    • Human-in-the-loop
  4. 4Evaluation and safetyWeeks 13 to 17+

    Know whether your agent is right before your users do.

    • Test sets and LLM judges
    • Red-teaming
    • Guardrails
  5. 5Production and capstoneWeeks 18 to 24+

    Deploy, monitor and present a capstone agent.

    • Latency and cost budgets
    • Observability
    • Capstone review

Tools you will use: Python, PyTorch, LangGraph, PostgreSQL + pgvector, Docker, GitHub Actions

Projects you will build

  1. Project 1

    Grounded support agent

    An agent that answers customer questions from your product documents and cites its sources.

  2. Project 2

    Research assistant with tools

    Plans multi-step searches, calls APIs and writes a referenced brief.

  3. Project 3

    Evaluation harness

    Automated test suites and LLM judges that catch regressions before release.

  4. Project 4

    Capstone: an agent for your workflow

    Shipped, monitored and presented to a panel of faculty and industry mentors.

Questions about this program

Do I need a GPU?

No. Labs run on a laptop with 8 GB of RAM. Heavier exercises use cloud credits included in the fee.

Will I get an IIT Hyderabad certificate?

Yes. Learners who meet the attendance and assessment requirements receive a certificate issued by the Centre for Continuing Education, IIT Hyderabad.

Can I attend if I miss a live class?

Every live session is recorded and available on the learning platform within 24 hours.

Is there an instalment option?

Yes. You can pay in up to three instalments without extra charge. Details are in your offer letter.
