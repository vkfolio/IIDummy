About the program

Through Open to All Teaching, IIT Hyderabad streams selected courses live from its classrooms each semester. You attend the same lectures as IITH students and can take the course assessments for a certificate.

Questions about this program

Will I get an IIT Hyderabad certificate?

Yes. Learners who meet the attendance and assessment requirements receive a certificate issued by the Centre for Continuing Education, IIT Hyderabad.

Can I attend if I miss a live class?

Every live session is recorded and available on the learning platform within 24 hours.
